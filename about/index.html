A coffee club that travels by post.

Four friends, four cities. Each round one of us picks a coffee from a roaster we rate and posts a small bag to the other three, with a numbered card that explains it.

We each brew it our own way, rate it here and compare notes on a call or in voice notes. Over time it builds into a record of every coffee we've shared and where it came from.

The four of us

How a round works

  1. Someone picksOne or two coffees they love, from a roaster they rate. Turns rotate between the four of us.
  2. Bags go outA small bag to each city, with an A6 card: the coffee's number, origin, process and tasting notes.
  3. Everyone tastesBrew it however you like, then rate it: a score, what you tasted, how it felt and how you made it.
  4. We compareOn a call or in voice notes. The coffee joins the record and lights up its origin on the globe.

What's on the site

  • The recordEvery coffee by number, with its details and everyone's ratings
  • Rate a coffeeThe review form, also opened from the QR code on each card
  • The globeWhere we are, and where the coffees we've tried were grown
  • Card makerMakes the printed card that goes in each bag
