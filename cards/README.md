# Card maker

Roadmap step 1. Swiss-grid A6 coffee cards, four to an A4 sheet, exported with jsPDF.

- Manual entry form for every field, bag photo upload
- Number colour: picker plus the 3 dominant bag colours, darkened to at least 3:1 on white
- Details rows can be renamed or dropped per coffee
- Exact layout: see [`docs/card-design-spec.md`](../docs/card-design-spec.md)
- Reads and writes the shape in [`data/coffees.json`](../data/coffees.json)
