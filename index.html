Four cities, one bag at a time.

Each round one of us picks a coffee and posts a bag to the others in Cincinnati, New York, London and Barcelona. We taste it, rate it, and keep the record here.

The record

How a round works

  1. Someone picksOne or two coffees, from a roaster they rate.
  2. Bags go outA small bag to each city, with a numbered card.
  3. Everyone tastesScore it, note the brew and what stood out.
  4. We compareOn a call or in voice notes, then the next person picks.
